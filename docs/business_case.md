# BUSINESS CASE

### **TrendOne Co.**

#### **1. Business Context**
This project is a case study simulation of a fictional clothing company. **TrendOne** is a B2C e-commerce retailer that sells casual fashion items in the United States. Their mission is to offer a wide variety of clothing from multiple brands, while creating a fun and easy shopping experience for customers.

TrendOne wants to use its data to get to know its customers better. By doing so, they hope to improve the shopping experience through personalized recommendations, loyalty perks, email campaigns, and better customer retention. Currently, TrendOne is looking to launch a subscription feature that offers exciting perks.

To help understand their customers better, the data analyst team has been asked to run an analysis aimed at boosting revenue and building effective marketing strategies. TrendOne also wants to figure out what kind of ads work best for their target audience. This analysis will be super useful for various cross-functional teams and will serve as the main basis for their decision-making.

#### **2. Business Problem**

TrendOne has accumulated a rich dataset containing valuable customer demographics and transaction history. However, this data remains an untapped asset due to the absence of a structured analytical framework. Currently, the business faces several key challenges:

- **Underutilized Customer Data**: Transactional data is collected but not systematically analyzed to drive business growth.

- **Lack of Customer Segmentation**: Without clear segmentation, TrendOne cannot effectively identify high-value customers or tailor marketing efforts to different buyer personas.

- **Unclear Purchasing Patterns**: The company lacks deep visibility into purchasing behavior, product preferences, and repeat purchase frequency.

- **Suboptimal Engagement Strategies**: Marketing initiatives, loyalty programs, and subscription offers are implemented without a data-backed understanding of customer engagement and retention drivers.

To resolve these issues, TrendOne needs a comprehensive analytics strategy to turn raw data into actionable business insights.

#### **3. Business Areas**
1. Customer Value
2. Retention
3. Purchase Behavior
4. Customer Engagement
